Hire from Canada and Mexico without the H-1B lottery

TN status has no annual cap and no lottery. A Canadian citizen can apply at a U.S. port of entry or preclearance airport with your offer letter, and decisions are usually made the same day. A Mexican citizen applies for a TN visa at a U.S. consulate first. Approval covers up to three years and can be renewed.

What your offer letter needs

  • The job title and duties matching one USMCA category (for example Engineer, Computer Systems Analyst, Scientist, Management Consultant, Accountant).
  • Start date, length of the assignment (up to three years), and salary.
  • The degree or license the role requires, which the candidate must hold.

There is no USCIS petition or filing fee for Canadian candidates entering at the border. A Form I-129 petition is optional.

Before you apply

  • You need Canadian or Mexican citizenship. Permanent residence of either country does not qualify.
  • Your degree should match the category. A Computer Systems Analyst usually needs a bachelor's degree, or a post-secondary diploma plus three years of experience.
  • Bring the offer letter, degree, transcripts and proof of citizenship to the border or consulate.

TN categories that show up on this board

USMCA Appendix 1603.D.1 lists about 60 professions. These are the ones employers on this board hire for most. A "check" tag on a job means the title does not name the category directly, so the offer letter has to describe duties that match it.
